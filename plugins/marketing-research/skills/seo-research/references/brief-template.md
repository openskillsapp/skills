# Content brief template

```markdown
# Brief: <query> — <market>, <YYYY-MM-DD>

## Goal
- Primary query: <query>. Related queries to cover: <list>.
- Searcher: <who, what they're trying to decide or do>.
- Page type: <the type that wins this results page>, because <evidence>.
- Conversion: <what the reader should do next on the user's site>.

## What ranks now
| # | Page | Type | Domain rating | Length | Strength | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

SERP features: <answer box / AI Overview (sources it cites) / PAA / videos>.

## The angle
<One paragraph: how this page beats the current top results, e.g. first-
hand testing they lack, a comparison table none of them has, fresher data,
a tool, a narrower audience.>

## Outline
- H1: <title>
- Direct answer in the first 2-3 sentences: <what it must say>
- H2: <section> — <what it covers, which ranking pages cover it, what to add>
- ...
- FAQ: <People Also Ask questions to answer, verbatim>

## Must include
- <facts, numbers, examples, visuals or tables the top pages share>
- <what none of them has that this page will>

## Proof and sources
- <the user's own data, customers, screenshots, expert quotes to gather>

## On-page
- Title tag (under 60 characters): ...
- Meta description (under 155): ...
- URL: /...
- Internal links: from <pages>, to <pages>
- Schema: <Article / FAQPage / HowTo / Product, as fits>

## Length
<Range from the top pages; cover the topic fully rather than hitting a
number.>
```

Keep the brief to what a writer needs. Quote headings from ranking pages
only to show coverage; never ask the writer to copy them.
